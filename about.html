About MOVEQUEST

A research-based learning platform designed to improve basic dance skills among BPED students through structured lessons and interactive assessments.

Our Mission

MOVEQUEST was developed to address the need for a structured, accessible, and research-based approach to improving basic dance skills among Bachelor of Physical Education (BPED) students.

The platform combines instructional videos, guided practice activities, knowledge assessments, and performance evaluations to create a comprehensive learning experience that students can follow at their own pace.

By leveraging technology, MOVEQUEST provides an engaging and effective way for students to develop their coordination, rhythm, posture, footwork, and choreographic understanding.

What We Offer

A complete learning system designed to develop your dance skills progressively from beginner to advanced level.

Video Lessons

Step-by-step instructional dance videos demonstrating proper techniques and movements for each skill area.

Structured Modules

Seven organized learning modules that progressively build from basic posture to complete choreography.

Guided Practice

Hands-on practice activities that strengthen coordination, rhythm, posture, and footwork skills.

ARSC Resources

Explore ARSC claps, animation songs, and videos in different languages to support your learning.

Learn at Your Own Pace

No sign-ups, no quizzes, and no pressure — browse the modules in any order and take your time with each one.

Dance Culture Insights

Discover the background, meaning, and cultural context behind the dances and songs featured in the resources.

The 7 Learning Modules

Each module focuses on a specific dance skill, building upon the previous one to create a complete learning pathway.

Module 1

Rhythm and Timing

Move in time with the beat of the music as the foundation of every dance performance.

Module 2

Coordination

Move different body parts together smoothly and efficiently.

Module 3

Balance

Maintain body stability while standing, turning, or changing positions.

Module 4

Body Control

Perform movements with proper posture, precision, and control.

Module 5

Footwork and Basic Steps

Perform the fundamental steps and patterns of a particular dance.

Module 6

Spatial Awareness

Understand directions, levels, and the space used when dancing.

Module 7

Movement Sequencing

Perform dance steps in the correct order with confidence and mastery.
