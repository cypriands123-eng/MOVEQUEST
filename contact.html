Frequently Asked Questions

What is MOVEQUEST?

MOVEQUEST is a research-based learning platform designed to improve basic dance skills among BPED students through structured lessons, quizzes, and progress tracking.

Who can use MOVEQUEST?

MOVEQUEST is designed for Bachelor of Physical Education (BPED) students, particularly those at Consolatrix College of Toledo City Inc.

How do I get started?

Simply create a free account by clicking the Register button, then start with Lesson 1 and work your way through the modules at your own pace.

Do I need to complete lessons in order?

Yes, each lesson builds upon the previous one. You need to pass each quiz with at least 75% to unlock the next lesson.

Is MOVEQUEST free to use?

Yes, MOVEQUEST is completely free for all BPED students.
