Lesson 1

Body Posture

Learn the correct body alignment, posture, and balance that serve as the foundation of every dance movement.

⏱ Duration

10 Minutes

📊 Difficulty

Beginner

🎯 Module

Dance Fundamentals

Lesson Video

Lesson Notes

Proper posture provides stability, improves balance, prevents injury, and allows dancers to perform movements gracefully and confidently.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify proper standing posture.
  • Maintain body alignment.
  • Develop balance.
  • Perform movements with confidence.

💡 Remember

Keep your head up, shoulders relaxed, chest lifted, core engaged, and knees slightly relaxed.

Practice Activity

Stand in front of a mirror for five minutes and maintain the correct dance posture while practicing simple arm and foot movements.

Reflection

How does maintaining proper posture improve your dance performance?
