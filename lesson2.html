Lesson 2

Coordination

Move different body parts together smoothly and efficiently while dancing.

⏱ Duration

12 Minutes

📊 Difficulty

Beginner

🎯 Module

Dance Fundamentals

Lesson Video

Learning Objectives

  • Define coordination in dance.
  • Combine movements of different body parts.
  • Perform a simple movement pattern smoothly and accurately.

Understanding Coordination

Coordination is the ability to organize and control different body movements so that they work together effectively to complete a task. In dance, coordination involves combining actions of the arms, legs, trunk, and head while maintaining the intended rhythm and movement pattern (Krasnow & Wilmerding, 2015).

Dance education research also identifies coordination as an important component of movement control and motor-skill development (Turan & Turan, 2026).

Why It Matters

Coordination enables dancers to combine steps, arm gestures, turns, and changes in direction without unnecessary pauses or conflicting movements. It supports the smooth execution of dance routines and helps students perform more complex movements as their skills develop.

Practice Activity: Opposite Arm and Leg

  1. Stand upright with feet comfortably apart.
  2. Step forward with the right foot while moving the left arm forward.
  3. Return to the starting position.
  4. Repeat with the left foot and right arm.
  5. Practise slowly before increasing the tempo.
  6. Add a four-count pattern and repeat it with music.

Focus on accuracy first and speed second. Practising a movement carefully before increasing its difficulty is consistent with motor-learning principles discussed by Krasnow and Wilmerding (2015).

Assessment

Perform an eight-count routine combining arm and leg movements. Assess whether you coordinate the body parts, follow the sequence correctly, and move smoothly without losing control.

References

  • Krasnow, D., & Wilmerding, M. V. (2015). Motor Learning and Control for Dance: Principles and Practices for Performers and Teachers. Human Kinetics. https://us.humankinetics.com/products/motor-learning-and-control-for-dance
  • Turan, A., & Turan, Z. (2026). “Dance Education from a Movement Science Perspective: A Systematic Review.” Frontiers in Psychology. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1931211/abstract

💡 Remember

Move slowly at first. Coordination improves through careful, repeated practice before speed is added.

Reflection

How does combining arm and leg movements change the way you control your body?
