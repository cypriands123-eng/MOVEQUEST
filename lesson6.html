Lesson 6

Guided Practice

Apply everything you have learned through guided practice activities. Strengthen your confidence, accuracy, and consistency while performing dance sequences.

⏱ Duration

25 Minutes

📊 Difficulty

Intermediate

🎯 Module

Skill Application

Lesson Video

Lesson Notes

Guided practice is a structured learning process where dancers perform movements with step-by-step guidance. It allows learners to receive immediate feedback, improve technique, and build confidence before performing independently.

Learning Objectives

  • Combine posture, rhythm, coordination, and footwork.
  • Follow guided dance demonstrations accurately.
  • Improve confidence through repeated practice.
  • Develop consistency in movement execution.

💡 Practice Tip

Practice each dance sequence slowly before increasing your speed. Focus on accuracy first, then gradually improve your performance.

Practice Activity

Follow the instructor throughout the entire dance routine. Pause the video whenever necessary, then continue until you can perform the complete routine without stopping.
