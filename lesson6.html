Lesson 6

Spatial Awareness

Understand directions, levels, and the space used when dancing.

⏱ Duration

25 Minutes

📊 Difficulty

Intermediate

🎯 Module

Skill Application

Lesson Video

Learning Objectives

  • Explain spatial awareness in dance.
  • Identify personal space, general space, and direction.
  • Move safely while maintaining appropriate distance from others.

Understanding Spatial Awareness

Spatial awareness is the ability to recognize and respond to one’s body position and the surrounding space during movement. In dance, it includes understanding direction, distance, pathways, levels, and the position of other dancers in relation to oneself (Krasnow & Wilmerding, 2015; Mogge et al., 2023).

Dance education connects movement with bodily awareness, physical experience, and the ability to understand and communicate through movement (Mogge et al., 2023). Krasnow and Wilmerding (2015) discuss motor control and sensory information relevant to movement in space.

Why It Matters

Spatial awareness helps dancers avoid collisions, maintain suitable spacing, and move through different pathways. It is especially important in group performances, where dancers must coordinate their positions and directions.

Practice Activity: Move Through Space

  1. Identify your personal space while standing in one spot.
  2. Walk forward, backward, sideways, and diagonally when instructed.
  3. Move at a low, medium, and high level using safe movements.
  4. Follow a straight, curved, or zigzag pathway.
  5. Practise moving around classmates while maintaining a safe distance.
  6. Finish by arranging the group in a simple line or formation.

Move at a controlled pace and keep enough space between yourself and others.

Assessment

Perform a short pattern that includes two directions, one change of level, and a pathway. Assess your ability to follow spatial instructions, maintain spacing, and move safely.

References

  • Krasnow, D., & Wilmerding, M. V. (2015). Motor Learning and Control for Dance: Principles and Practices for Performers and Teachers. Human Kinetics. https://us.humankinetics.com/products/motor-learning-and-control-for-dance
  • Mogge, S. G., Huggins, S., Knutson, J., Lobel, E. E., & Segal, P. (Eds.). (2023). Multiple Literacies for Dance, Physical Education and Sports. Springer. https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-031-20117-2

💡 Remember

Always be aware of the dancers around you. Use your space safely and deliberately.
