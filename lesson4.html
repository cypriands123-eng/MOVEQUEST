Lesson 4

Body Control

Perform movements with proper posture, precision, and control.

⏱ Duration

18 Minutes

📊 Difficulty

Beginner

🎯 Module

Dance Fundamentals

Lesson Video

Learning Objectives

  • Explain body control in dance.
  • Demonstrate controlled posture and movement.
  • Perform basic dance actions with appropriate force, direction, and precision.

Understanding Body Control

Body control is the ability to regulate body position, muscle action, and movement to perform a physical task with control and precision. In dance, it involves maintaining posture, managing the speed and force of movements, and transitioning smoothly between actions (Krasnow & Wilmerding, 2015; Grossman, 2015).

Turan and Turan (2026) also identify body awareness and movement control as important themes in dance education research.

Why It Matters

Body control helps dancers perform movements accurately, maintain appropriate posture, and avoid unnecessary or exaggerated actions. It also supports smoother transitions between steps and helps dancers adjust their movements to match the demands of a routine.

Practice Activity: Move, Freeze, and Control

  1. Stand upright with relaxed shoulders and knees.
  2. Walk in place for eight counts.
  3. On the next count, freeze in a stable dance pose.
  4. Hold the position without unnecessary movement.
  5. Continue with a slow arm movement and a controlled turn.
  6. Repeat the sequence, focusing on posture and smooth transitions.

Practise slowly before attempting faster movements. Motor-learning principles emphasize practising and refining movements to improve performance (Krasnow & Wilmerding, 2015).

Assessment

Perform a short sequence containing walking, an arm gesture, a pause, and a turn. Assess your posture, precision, control of movement, and ability to transition between actions.

References

  • Grossman, G. (2015). Dance Science: Anatomy, Movement Analysis, Conditioning. Princeton Book Company. https://www.ipgbook.com/dance-science-products-9780871273888.php
  • Krasnow, D., & Wilmerding, M. V. (2015). Motor Learning and Control for Dance: Principles and Practices for Performers and Teachers. Human Kinetics. https://us.humankinetics.com/products/motor-learning-and-control-for-dance
  • Turan, A., & Turan, Z. (2026). “Dance Education from a Movement Science Perspective: A Systematic Review.” Frontiers in Psychology. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1931211/abstract

💡 Remember

Keep movements deliberate. Control your speed and force rather than rushing through each action.

Reflection

How does controlling the speed and force of your movements improve your accuracy?
