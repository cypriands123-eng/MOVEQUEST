Lesson 3

Balance

Maintain body stability while standing, turning, or changing positions.

⏱ Duration

15 Minutes

📊 Difficulty

Beginner

🎯 Module

Dance Fundamentals

Lesson Video

Learning Objectives

  • Explain balance in dance.
  • Maintain a stable body position during simple movements.
  • Demonstrate controlled weight shifts and basic balance positions.

Understanding Balance

Balance is the ability to maintain or regain body stability while standing still or moving. In dance, balance involves controlling body position and weight distribution during poses, steps, turns, and transitions (Krasnow & Wilmerding, 2015; Grossman, 2015).

Balance is a relevant component of dance movement and physical performance. Research reviewing dance education from a movement-science perspective also identifies balance among the motor skills addressed in dance instruction (Turan & Turan, 2026).

Why It Matters

Balance helps dancers maintain stable positions, control their movements, and transition safely from one step to another. It is especially useful when performing on one foot, changing direction, or holding a pose.

Practice Activity: The Balance Challenge

  1. Stand with both feet on the floor.
  2. Shift your weight slowly from one foot to the other.
  3. Lift one foot slightly and maintain a stable position for five seconds.
  4. Lower the foot carefully and repeat on the other side.
  5. Practise holding a simple dance pose.
  6. Progress to a slow step-and-pause sequence.

Use a clear, uncluttered space and avoid challenging balance positions beyond your ability.

Assessment

Perform a simple sequence involving a weight shift, a one-foot balance, and a final pose. Assess your stability, posture, control, and ability to transition safely.

References

  • Krasnow, D., & Wilmerding, M. V. (2015). Motor Learning and Control for Dance: Principles and Practices for Performers and Teachers. Human Kinetics. https://us.humankinetics.com/products/motor-learning-and-control-for-dance
  • Grossman, G. (2015). Dance Science: Anatomy, Movement Analysis, Conditioning. Princeton Book Company. https://www.ipgbook.com/dance-science-products-9780871273888.php
  • Turan, A., & Turan, Z. (2026). “Dance Education from a Movement Science Perspective: A Systematic Review.” Frontiers in Psychology. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1931211/abstract

💡 Remember

Keep your core engaged and your gaze steady to hold balance positions more confidently.

Reflection
