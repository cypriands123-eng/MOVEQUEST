Lesson 3

Coordination

Improve your ability to control different parts of your body simultaneously to perform smooth, balanced, and accurate dance movements.

⏱ Duration

15 Minutes

📊 Difficulty

Beginner

🎯 Module

Dance Fundamentals

Lesson Video

Lesson Notes

Coordination is the ability to move different parts of the body together smoothly and efficiently. Good coordination helps dancers perform movements with better balance, control, and confidence while reducing unnecessary body tension.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the importance of body coordination in dance.
  • Develop upper and lower body coordination.
  • Perform movements smoothly and accurately.
  • Improve balance and movement control.

💡 Remember

Relax your body while dancing. Focus on moving your arms, legs, and body together naturally instead of forcing every movement.

Practice Activity

Stand in front of a mirror and practice stepping side to side while swinging your arms in rhythm. Repeat the exercise several times until your movements become smooth and coordinated.
