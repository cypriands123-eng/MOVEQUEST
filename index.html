Improve Your Basic Dance Skills with MOVEQUEST

MOVEQUEST is a research-based learning platform designed to improve the basic dance skills of Bachelor of Physical Education (BPED) students through structured lessons, instructional videos, guided practice, performance submissions, and knowledge assessments.

Why Choose MOVEQUEST?

Everything you need to improve your basic dance skills through a structured learning experience.

Video Lessons

Watch step-by-step instructional dance videos that demonstrate proper techniques and movements.

Structured Lessons

Learn basic dance skills through organized learning modules arranged from beginner to advanced.

Practice Activities

Strengthen your coordination, rhythm, posture, and footwork through guided practice activities.

Knowledge Checks

Complete Google Forms quizzes after every lesson to evaluate your understanding.

Progress Tracking

Keep track of completed lessons, submitted performances, and overall learning progress.

Performance Assessment

Submit your recorded dance performance through Google Forms for instructor evaluation using a standardized rubric.
