Lesson 5

Footwork and Basic Steps

Perform the fundamental steps and patterns of a particular dance.

⏱ Duration

20 Minutes

📊 Difficulty

Intermediate

🎯 Module

Core Dance Skills

Lesson Video

Learning Objectives

  • Explain the meaning of footwork and basic dance steps.
  • Demonstrate selected basic steps using proper technique.
  • Combine basic steps into a short dance pattern.

Understanding Footwork and Basic Steps

Footwork refers to the placement, movement, and coordination of the feet during physical activity. In dance, basic steps are foundational movement patterns that dancers practise and combine to create longer sequences or routines (Krasnow & Wilmerding, 2015; Andin, 2004).

Krasnow and Wilmerding (2015) discuss motor skills, movement patterns, and the principles involved in learning dance techniques. Andin (2004) provides a Philippine educational perspective on dance education and movement instruction.

Why It Matters

Footwork provides the foundation for travelling, changing direction, shifting weight, and performing more complex dance patterns. Learning basic steps first helps students develop technique before combining movements into longer sequences.

Practice Activity: Step, Close, and Repeat

  1. Stand with feet comfortably apart.
  2. Step to the right with the right foot.
  3. Bring the left foot toward the right foot.
  4. Repeat the pattern to the left.
  5. Practise the pattern slowly while counting from 1 to 8.
  6. Repeat with music, keeping the steps controlled and consistent.

Teachers may introduce other beginner-level steps, such as step-touch, marching, and simple forward and backward steps, depending on students’ abilities.

Assessment

Perform a short combination of basic steps in both directions. Assess correct foot placement, weight transfer, rhythm, and consistency.

References

  • Andin, C. T. (2004). Dance Education in the School Curriculum. Rex Book Store. Philippine eLib catalogue: https://www.elib.gov.ph/results.php?f=subject&q=Dance+--+Study+and+teaching+%28Higher%29
  • Krasnow, D., & Wilmerding, M. V. (2015). Motor Learning and Control for Dance: Principles and Practices for Performers and Teachers. Human Kinetics. https://us.humankinetics.com/products/motor-learning-and-control-for-dance

💡 Remember

Land softly and shift your weight smoothly to keep your footwork clean and balanced.
